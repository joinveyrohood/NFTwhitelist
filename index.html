Whitelist Allocation

VeyroHood has two allocation stages.

OG — First 1,000

The first 1,000 successfully verified participants receive OG status and can mint their allocated NFT using the same wallet used for verification.

FCFS — Remaining 9,000

After the OG allocation, the remaining 9,000 NFTs are available through the FCFS allocation for eligible participants.
