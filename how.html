RULES

HOW THE HOOD WORKS.

SUPPLY

10,000 memberships. First 1,000 verified wallets are OG. The rest wait for whitelist.

ENTRY

Finish missions, then pay $0.30 USDG from your wallet. Confirmation happens on-chain.

ONE WALLET

One wallet, one seat. Missions without payment do not count as OG.

REFERRAL

Your private link appears on the dashboard after you are verified. You earn when someone you invited also verifies.

CLAIM

When your available reward reaches $2 you can request payout from the dashboard.

MINT

Mint opens later for allocated wallets. Still one NFT max.
