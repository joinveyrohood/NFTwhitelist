HOW THE HOOD WORKS.

SUPPLY

10,000 NFTs. OG #1–#1000 by paid verification order. WL #1001–#10000 by random + X performance. Counters never reset.

PAYMENT

$0.30 USDG to 0xf6F80827cBAf83798c7763FCd915C0068F2bE60C on Ethereum (1) or Robinhood (4663). Worker verifies receipt.

ONE WALLET

One wallet can hold one allocation. Duplicate tx hashes are rejected. Missions without payment do not create OG.

REFERRAL

After paid verify you get ?ref=CODE. 20% = $0.06 to referrer, 80% = $0.24 treasury. Same chain accounting. Idempotent ledger.

CLAIM

At $2+ tap Claim All. Balance goes pending. Admin reviews, sends USDG, pastes payout tx. You pay gas.

MINT

Mint contract is separate. After a mint tx exists, record it so allocation status becomes minted. Still one NFT max.
